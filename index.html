Questions, answered.

What is Source?

Source is a free trade tool for Sleeper fantasy football leagues. It reads your league, measures what every team needs at each starting slot, and builds trades that raise both teams' starting points. It also drafts the pitch, lineup fixes and waiver bids.

What kind of tool is Source?

A fantasy trade calculator. Source is here to help you get better outcomes on trades and set the optimal lineup for your team. It doesn't support prop bets or betting of any kind. Vegas lines only help shape its point projections.

Is Source free?

Yes. Every screen is free, from trade ideas to waiver bids. Connect your Sleeper league with just your username.

Do you need my Sleeper password?

No. Source only needs your Sleeper username. League data comes from Sleeper's public API, so there's nothing to sign into.

Can Source make moves in my league?

No. Source is read only and can't change anything in your Sleeper league. It drafts offers, lineup changes and waiver bids. You make every move yourself.

Where do trade values come from?

Source blends public market values for your league's scoring format with a rest-of-season projection model and each team's actual starting needs. Then every offer is judged by what it does to both starting lineups.

How does Ask work?

Source AI answers questions about your league from its rosters, standings and matchups. Trade math comes from Source's own engine. Once in a while it cracks a joke. Past the daily limit, Ask gives the built-in answer.

What does Set optimal lineup check?

It starts from your best projected lineup, then reads player news, injury and practice reports, Vegas lines and kickoff weather. Every suggested move comes with its reason. You make the moves in Sleeper.

Does a pitch send on its own?

No. Source writes the pitch and draws the share image in your browser. Nothing reaches the other manager until you share, copy or text it yourself.

Do sent offers sync across devices?

Yes, in a connected Sleeper league. Offers you send and players you star on Waivers sync to your phone, tablet and desktop. A key on your devices protects them. A new device joins with a one-time code from Settings. The sample league stays on one device.

Will other managers see my notes?

No. Tags and notes you log about league mates stay on your device. Source never shows them to other managers.

What data does Source keep?

Tags, prices, settings and history stay in your browser. Sent offers and starred players are saved for syncing, and only your device key can read them. Delete my data in Settings clears this browser and the synced copies.

Does the win chance match Sleeper?

Closely. Source uses the lineup you set in Sleeper and a model tuned to Sleeper's win probability, refreshed every 15 seconds while games are on.

Which platforms work?

Source works with Sleeper today. Yahoo and ESPN are next, then MyFantasyLeague and CBS.
