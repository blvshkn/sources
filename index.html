Questions, answered.

What is Source?

Source is a free trade tool for Sleeper fantasy football leagues. It reads your league, measures what every team needs at each starting slot, and builds trades that raise both teams' starting points. It also drafts the pitch, lineup fixes and waiver bids.

Is Source free?

Yes. Source is free. Connect your Sleeper league with your username and use every screen, from trade ideas to waiver bids, at no cost.

Do you need my Sleeper password?

No. Source only needs your Sleeper username. League data comes from Sleeper's public API, so there's no password and nothing to sign into.

Can Source make moves in my league?

No. Source is read only, so it can't change anything in your Sleeper league. It drafts offers, lineup changes and waiver bids. You make every move in Sleeper yourself.

Where do trade values come from?

Source blends public market values for your league's scoring format with a projection model for the rest of the season and each team's actual starting needs. Every offer is then judged by what it does to both starting lineups.

How does Ask work?

Source AI answers questions about your league from its rosters, standings and matchups. Trade math comes from Source' own engine, and once in a while the AI cracks a joke. There's a daily limit, and past it Ask gives the built-in answer.

What does Set optimal lineup check?

It starts from your best projected lineup, then reads player news, injury and practice reports, Vegas lines and kickoff weather. Every suggested move comes with its reason. You make the moves in Sleeper.

Does a pitch send on its own?

No. Source writes the pitch and draws the share image in your browser. Nothing reaches the other manager until you share, copy or text it yourself.

Do sent offers sync across devices?

Yes, in a connected Sleeper league. Offers you send and players you star on Waivers sync to your phone, tablet and desktop. A key that lives on your devices protects them, and a new device joins with a one-time code from Settings. The sample league stays on one device.

Will other managers see my notes?

No. Tags and notes you log about league mates stay on your device. Source never shows them to other managers.

What data does Source keep?

Tags, prices, settings and history stay in your browser. Sent offers and starred players are saved so they can sync, readable only with your device key. Delete my data in Settings clears this browser and the synced copies.

Does the win chance match Sleeper?

Closely. Source uses the lineup you set in Sleeper and a model tuned to Sleeper's win probability, refreshed every 15 seconds while games are on.

Which platforms work?

Source works with Sleeper today. Yahoo and ESPN are next, then MyFantasyLeague and CBS.
